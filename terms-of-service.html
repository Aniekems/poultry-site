2. Purpose of This Site

The Poultry Tip Site is an educational resource that provides practical, field-oriented information on broiler chicken production. Topics include housing, brooding, feeding, feed planning, vaccination, disease awareness, marketing, common management mistakes, and the comparison between broilers and layers.

The content is written for poultry farmers of all experience levels who want clear, practical, and actionable guidance drawn from real farming experience.

3. Using This Site’s Guidance Responsibly

The guides on this Site are built from practical broiler-farming experience and are meant to help you manage your flock with confidence. Many routine tasks — including brooding, feeding, vaccination through drinking water, and preventive medication — are well within the reach of a well-informed farmer, and this Site shows you how.

Some situations need more than a guide. Contact a licensed veterinarian or your state veterinary officer if:

  • you suspect avian influenza, or you see sudden high mortality;
  • birds keep dying after you have treated them;
  • you cannot identify the disease;
  • you are unsure about a drug, a dose, or whether two products can be mixed.

Every farm differs in climate, strain, feed, disease pressure and management, so results will vary. The information here is general education and does not replace a veterinarian’s diagnosis of your specific flock.
