About The Poultry Tip Site

The Poultry Tip Site explains how to raise broilers from day-old chicks to market, in plain language, for beginners and experienced farmers alike.

Every guide follows the order of a real production cycle: build the house, brood the chicks, feed them, vaccinate on time, spot disease early, and sell at a price that covers your costs. You can read the guides one after another before your first batch, or jump to the page you need in the middle of a cycle.

Why this site exists

Broiler farming looks simple, but small decisions add up: how warm the brooding house is, whether a vaccine is given on the right day, how dry the litter stays, whether feed was costed before the chicks arrived, and whether a buyer is waiting when the birds are ready. Getting these right goes a long way toward a good batch and helps you avoid costly ones.

This site covers broiler farming from start to finish: housing, brooding, feeding, feed planning, vaccination, disease control and marketing, along with a guide to common beginner mistakes and a comparison of broilers and layers. Where it helps, the guides include schedules, tables and worked examples you can apply to your own flock.

What you will find here

  • Housing and equipment

    Site selection, brooding house and rearing pen design, ventilation, litter, feeders, drinkers and heat sources.

  • Brooding

    Heat sources, reading chick behaviour, and the safety rules for charcoal pots and lanterns.

  • Feeding

    Super starter, starter and finisher feed, the day-26 to day-29 transition, and 5–6 week versus 8–10 week programmes.

  • Feed planning

    How to turn a target weight and feed-conversion ratio into a number of bags, with a worked example.

  • Vaccination

    The Gumboro and Lasota schedule, how to vaccinate through drinking water, and how much water to use.

  • Broiler diseases

    Signs, prevention and treatment of the common diseases, plus a day-by-day medication schedule.

  • Marketing

    Finding buyers before the chicks arrive, and calculating your cost per bird and a starting price.

  • Broilers vs layers

    A side-by-side comparison of capital, timeline, management and risk to help you pick a starting point.

  • Common mistakes

    The management errors that cost beginners the most, and how to avoid each one.

New to broilers? Start with the home page overview, then read Common Mistakes before your chicks arrive.

How we write our guides

Drawn from field experience
The guidance comes from hands-on poultry farming and focuses on what to do at each stage and what to watch for.
Real examples
We name actual strains, feed types and common drugs, so you know what to look for when you buy.
Numbers you can use
Where a figure varies, we give a realistic range, and the feed planning and pricing guides show you how to calculate your own figures from your farm records.
